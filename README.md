# Sales-Forecasting-And-Customer-Segmentation-Dashboard
This project analyzes transactional sales data from a U.S.-based coffee company (2023–2024) to uncover revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, and future sales projections.

The dashboard leverages Power BI’s data modeling and forecasting capabilities to deliver actionable insights that support strategic decision-making across sales performance, customer segmentation, and regional analysis.
## Objectives
Evaluate overall business performance (Revenue, Transactions, Customers).

Identify top-performing products and product categories.

Analyze sales trends across daily and monthly timeframes.

Assess regional revenue distribution across U.S. states.

Perform customer segmentation using RFM analysis.

Implement time-series forecasting with seasonality (12-month cycle).

Support data-driven business decision-making.
## Dataset Information

Format: CSV

Time Period: 2023 – 2024

Geographical Scope: United States

## Key Features
### Revenue Analysis
Monthly and category-level revenue breakdown

Daily and monthly trend analysis
### Customer Segmentation (RFM)
Customers categorized into segments such as:

Loyal Customers

At Risk

New Customers

Occasional Buyers
### Forecasting
Implemented using Power BI Analytics (Exponential Smoothing)

Configured with 12-month seasonality

Provides forward-looking revenue projections
### Geographic Analysis
State-level revenue distribution

Identification of high-performing regions
## Key Insights
Coffee products are the primary revenue drivers, significantly outperforming other categories

Sales exhibit consistent patterns with mild seasonal variation, indicating stable demand

A relatively small customer base contributes a large share of revenue, suggesting high customer value concentration

Forecasting indicates steady future performance with no extreme volatility

Loyal customers contribute the highest revenue share, highlighting the importance of retention strategies

Underperforming categories (Tea, Pastry) present opportunities for bundling and upselling

Regional disparities suggest targeted marketing opportunities in lower-performing states
